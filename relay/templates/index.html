DroneRaceView Relay - Overlays

Notes

This relay serves the overlay pages and snapshot data over Socket.IO. Live race events (countdowns, staging) are not relayed - some pages ship with non-animating timing visuals by design.


Pilot Avatars
Avatars are read from the relay's /shared/avatars/ mount. Drop <callsign>.jpg (lowercase, spaces -> underscores, ideally 256x256 or 512x512) into the host's ./shared/avatars/ directory. Missing files fall back to no_avatar.png.


Country Flags
Set a pilot's country attribute on the timer; the flag icon appears before the callsign. Flag images come from flagpedia.net.
